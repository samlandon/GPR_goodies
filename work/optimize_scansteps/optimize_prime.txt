Equation for Max Iterations: 
Iter = (Axis_length/Step_size - 10dx - Pos_initial)
(dx = #steps = dx_dy_dz/Step_size)
(Pos_initial = #steps = position_of_rx_or_src_which_is_farthest/Step_size)

Equation for Max Step Size:   << not set in stone, yet
Dist_from_end = Axis_length - Pos_initial
Max_step_size = Dist_from_end - Pos_initial
(grab total distance from start to same relative place from end and make it all one step)
